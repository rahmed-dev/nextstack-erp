# Product Intro and Idea

**One-liner**  
Local-first business OS for solo freelancers (and tiny agencies) that runs offline, syncs via Google Drive, and covers lead → proposal → contract → invoice → payment → reporting.

**Target users**  
Individual freelancers who want ownership of their data, offline reliability, and fast daily workflows across mobile and desktop.

**Problem/need**  
- Cloud CRMs and invoicing tools feel heavy, go offline, or lock data away.  
- Collections and retainers are painful to manage manually.  
- Mobile capture is slow; desktop productivity is fragmented.  
- Privacy-conscious users want Drive-based sync and optional end-to-end encryption.

**Core promise**  
Own your data, work anywhere, and get paid faster: capture on mobile in seconds, formalize scope with contracts, invoice confidently, automate collections, and see cash clarity without losing offline control.

**Differentiators**  
- Offline-first SQLite on every device; sync via Drive with append-only ops log.  
- Trust and ownership: user-held data, optional E2E encryption, portable exports.  
- Collections and retainers as a wedge: reminders, retainer ledger, statements.  
- Lightweight “DocType-like” customization without server complexity.  
- Mobile-first capture with a calm, big-number dashboard style.

**Success outcomes**  
- Activation: first lead + first invoice + first payment within 48 hours.  
- Conversion lift: overdue reduced; collection reminders improve on-time payments.  
- Reliability: conflict-safe sync and immutable accounting entries.  
- Revenue: paid conversion to Insights tier; retention via daily-use workflows.
