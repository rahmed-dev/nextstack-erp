# Product Brief: NextStack ERP

**Date:** 2025-12-08
**Author:** Riz

## Executive Summary

NextStack ERP is a lightweight business cockpit for individual freelancers and small agencies who mostly live inside Upwork but lack a proper way to track their leads, proposals, projects, and finances in one place. Instead of juggling everything inside Upwork and memory, or setting up heavy tools, NextStack ERP gives them a unified, low-effort workspace to run their business.

The product focuses on three outcomes:
- Never losing track of leads, proposals, or what was agreed with a client.
- Having a clear view of active clients, active projects, and how much money is actually being earned and spent.
- Making business decisions based on simple, useful insights—not time-consuming admin.

The app is designed to be efficient and non-burdensome: roughly 1–4 hours of data entry per week per person (or less), with Upwork as the main source of truth plus Excel import/export for other work. A unified database powers both a mobile app (for quick expense entry and checking pending work) and a desktop app (the full “playground” for deeper analysis and planning).

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Individual freelancers and small agencies often rely almost entirely on Upwork to manage their work. They don’t systematically track leads and proposals outside Upwork, which means they frequently lose track of:
- Who they should follow up with.
- What a lead asked for.
- What was agreed to deliver for each project.

At the same time, there is no unified view of:
- How many active clients and projects they have.
- Which projects are earning the most in the last few weeks.
- How much income is coming in versus expenses, and what their current net worth looks like.

For small agencies, these problems become more complex with:
- Multiple client relationships to juggle.
- Team coordination around tasks and deliverables.
- Multi-member finances and tracking who is doing what, for which client.

The result is lost opportunities, confusion about priorities, and a constant feeling of “I might be missing something important.”

### Problem Impact

Because leads and proposals are not tracked outside Upwork, important opportunities can quietly die: no follow-up, no clear memory of what the prospect wanted, and no easy way to see which leads are still warm. Work agreements can be forgotten or buried in messages, leading to scope confusion and rework.

On the financial side, freelancers and small agencies struggle to answer basic questions:
- How much did I actually earn this month?
- What did I spend, and on what categories?
- Which projects make the most money in the last few weeks?
- How is my overall net worth evolving?

Without clear answers, it’s hard to improve pricing, decide which clients to double down on, or plan for growth. Admin tasks also risk becoming a burden—time spent on spreadsheets instead of billable work.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Today, most freelancers and small agencies either:
- Live entirely in Upwork and use it as an accidental “system of record”, or
- Patch together spreadsheets, note-taking tools, and generic project management apps.

These approaches have gaps:
- Upwork is great for finding and running jobs, but not for:
  - Tracking leads and proposals in a structured way.
  - Seeing a consolidated view of work, sales, and accounting.
  - Understanding client history and repeated business at a glance.
- Spreadsheets and generic tools (Notion, ClickUp, etc.) require a lot of setup and ongoing manual updates. They are flexible, but they are not purpose-built for freelancers’ lead–project–accounting flow.
- Many accounting tools focus on bookkeeping but ignore leads, proposals, and project-level views, so they don’t help with day-to-day decisions about where to focus.

What’s missing is a simple, opinionated system that connects leads, projects, income, expenses, and insights in a way that matches how freelancers and small agencies actually work.

### Proposed Solution

NextStack ERP provides a unified, opinionated workflow for running a freelance or small agency business:

- **Daily dashboard**
  - Shows open leads that are scheduled for contact or follow-up.
  - Highlights pending tasks across current projects.
  - De-emphasizes invoicing (since it’s monthly or scheduled) so the daily view stays focused on execution and follow-up.

- **Weekly workflow with low data entry**
  - Designed for ~1–4 hours of data entry per week, not constant admin.
  - Upwork as a primary source of data, with Excel import/export to cover non-Upwork work without making the product feel incomplete.

- **Insights that drive better decisions**
  - Number of proposals submitted and patterns that may explain why they are or aren’t converting (e.g., which types tend to get interviews).
  - Number of active clients and active projects.
  - Which projects have been the highest earning in the last 3 weeks.
  - How many active leads exist and which clients provide repeated business.
  - An accounting dashboard with:
    - This month’s income and expense chart.
    - Expenses entered by category, summarized visually (e.g., pie chart).
    - A view of current net worth.

- **Project and task view**
  - A tree-structured project view showing projects, tasks, and their status in a clear hierarchy (similar to the example you shared), so it’s easy to see what’s in progress, what’s pending, and what’s next.

- **Unified database across devices**
  - A mobile app focused on quick actions:
    - Fast daily expense entry by category.
    - Quick view of pending work and leads.
  - A desktop app as the full “playground”:
    - Rich dashboards.
    - Deeper analysis using insights to make better business decisions.

Overall, NextStack ERP aims to be a supportive tool, not a burden—helping freelancers and small agencies manage their work, sales, and accounts efficiently so they can focus on doing great work and building toward financial freedom.

### Key Differentiators

- **Freelancer- and Upwork-first, not generic.** Designed around the reality of freelancers and small agencies who primarily use Upwork and similar channels, instead of trying to be a generic project or accounting tool.
- **Leads → Projects → Money in one flow.** Connects leads and proposals, active projects, client relationships, and financial outcomes (income, expenses, net worth) in a single system.
- **Low admin, weekly rhythm.** Optimized for 1–4 hours of data entry per week with simple workflows, instead of expecting constant manual updates.
- **Unified mobile + desktop experience.** One database powering:
  - Mobile for quick daily actions (expenses, checking tasks/leads).
  - Desktop for complete visibility and deeper business decisions.
- **Built by an actual freelancer for freelancers.** Grounded in real freelance experience: the desire to have a simple, unified platform instead of managing servers, VPSs, and a patchwork of tools—aiming toward both better operations and personal financial freedom.

## Target Users

### Primary Users

#### Persona 1: Independent Upwork Freelancer

**Profile**

- Works primarily through Upwork, with occasional side projects outside the platform.
- Typically offers services such as development or bookkeeping, but the product is designed to work for almost any freelance niche.
- Often working solo, responsible for finding clients, delivering work, and keeping basic financial records.
- Wants to focus on doing good work and earning more, not maintaining complicated tools or infrastructure.

**Problem Experience**

- Depends on Upwork as the main “system of record” for conversations, proposals, and contracts.
- Does not systematically track leads and proposals outside Upwork, so follow-ups are easy to miss.
- Sometimes loses track of what a lead wanted or what was agreed to deliver for a project.
- Lacks a single place to see active clients, active projects, and basic financial health (income vs expenses, recent earnings, net worth).

**Success Vision**

- Has a simple dashboard that shows open leads to contact and pending tasks for current projects.
- Spends at most 1–4 hours per week on data entry, not more.
- Can see at a glance:
  - How many proposals were submitted and how they are performing.
  - How many active clients and projects there are.
  - Which projects are highest earning in the last few weeks.
  - This month’s income and expenses, plus current net worth.
- Uses the mobile app for quick actions (checking pending work, adding expenses by category) and the desktop app as a full “playground” for deeper planning and insight.

#### Persona 2: Small Agency Owner (2–5 People)

**Profile**

- Runs a small agency offering services through Upwork and other channels (any service: development, design, consulting, etc.).
- Team might include the founder plus a few delivery team members and possibly a VA.
- Manages multiple client relationships at once, with parallel projects and shared finances.

**Problem Experience**

- Must juggle multiple clients, each with their own projects, timelines, and deliverables.
- Coordination is difficult: who is working on what, for which client, and what’s due next.
- Financial tracking is fragmented: agency revenue, expenses, and distributions to team members are not easily visible in one place.
- Upwork helps with finding and fulfilling work but doesn’t give an integrated view of leads, projects, and money.

**Success Vision**

- Has a unified view of all clients and projects, including project statuses and tasks in a tree-like structure.
- Can quickly see which projects are most profitable in recent weeks and which clients give repeated business.
- Uses NextStack ERP as the central place to coordinate work, track leads, and monitor agency-level income and expenses.
- Team members see only what they need (e.g., assigned tasks and relevant projects), while the owner sees the full business picture.

### Secondary Users

#### Persona 3: Virtual Assistant (VA)

**Role and Responsibilities**

- Helps the freelancer or agency owner with admin tasks related to leads and proposals.
- Enters or updates lead and proposal data in NextStack ERP (e.g., new leads from Upwork, status updates, notes on conversations).
- May help keep basic project or task information up to date.

**Access and Permissions**

- Needs access to lead, proposal, and possibly task-related doctypes.
- Should not see sensitive financial details such as full net worth or certain personal financial information.
- Works primarily on desktop but may occasionally use mobile for quick updates.

#### Persona 4: Bookkeeper / Accountant

**Role and Responsibilities**

- Supports the freelancer or agency in keeping financial data clean and ready for tax and reporting.
- Handles expense data entry and categorization.
- Prepares or exports reports from NextStack ERP that can be used for tax filing or external accounting tools.

**Access and Permissions**

- Needs access to income, expense, and report-related doctypes.
- May not need full access to all client communication or detailed project tasks.
- Likely works primarily on desktop, focusing on accurate financial records and clean exports.

### User Journey

**Discovery**

- Hears about NextStack ERP from other freelancers or agency owners who want something better than “Upwork + memory + spreadsheets”.
- May also find it while searching for tools to track freelance leads, projects, and finances in one place.

**Onboarding**

- Connects their existing work sources:
  - Starts with Upwork as the main channel.
  - Uses Excel import to bring in off-Upwork projects or financial data.
- Sets up a few basic structures:
  - Active leads and proposals.
  - Current projects and tasks (using a tree-style project view).
  - Initial income and expense categories.

**Core Usage**

- **Solo freelancer:**
  - Daily: opens the dashboard to see open leads scheduled for contact and pending tasks.
  - Weekly: spends 1–4 hours updating proposals, project statuses, and expenses by category.
  - Uses mobile for quick expense entry and checking what’s pending.

- **Small agency owner:**
  - Weekly: reviews active clients and projects, team assignments, and high-level financial metrics.
  - Coordinates work across team members using the project and task structure.
  - Uses reports to see which projects and clients are most valuable.

- **VA:**
  - Periodically logs into the system to enter or update leads and proposals.
  - Ensures lead and proposal data (especially from Upwork) stays current.

- **Bookkeeper:**
  - Periodically updates expenses and categories.
  - Generates reports or exports for tax preparation and external accounting.

**Success Moment**

- Realizes they no longer lose track of leads, proposals, or agreed deliverables.
- Can open one place to understand:
  - Who to follow up with.
  - What work is pending.
  - How the business is performing financially this month.
- Feels that the system is light enough to maintain, instead of becoming another burden.

**Long-term**

- NextStack ERP becomes their standard way of running the freelance or small agency business.
- Mobile + desktop together make it easy to keep data fresh without heavy effort.
- Over time, they use insights from the app to adjust which clients to focus on, how to price, and how to grow toward financial freedom.

## Success Metrics

For NextStack ERP, success is defined first by user outcomes (especially for you as the first user), and second by learning and validation as a small but real product.

### User Success Metrics

- **Lead Coverage**
  - At least **80–90% of active leads** are logged in the system with a clear status and next action.
  - Users rarely lose track of who to follow up with or what was discussed.

- **Work Habit / Usage**
  - Users open NextStack ERP **daily on workdays** to:
    - Check open leads scheduled for contact.
    - Review pending tasks across projects.
  - This daily usage is light-weight and focused on clarity, not heavy admin.

- **Accounting Habit**
  - Users perform **weekly accounting updates**, entering expenses by category and keeping income/expense data reasonably current.

- **Financial Review**
  - Users complete **at least one monthly review** inside the app where they:
    - See total income for the month.
    - See expenses by category (e.g., in a chart).
    - View an updated net worth snapshot.
  - Users feel more confident and organized about their finances compared to before using the product.

- **Perceived Value**
  - Users (including you) feel that:
    - The system helps them “get things organized” rather than becoming a burden.
    - The time spent on data entry (around 1–4 hours per week) feels worth it.

### Business Objectives

- **Learning & Product Experience**
  - You gain real experience designing, building, and running your own product end-to-end.
  - NextStack ERP becomes your primary tool for managing your own freelance business, giving you first-hand product feedback.

- **Early User Base**
  - Within the first 12 months, you have **1–5 active external users** (beyond yourself) who use the product regularly for their freelance or small agency work.
  - These users report that the product brings them clear value (especially around leads, organization, and financial clarity), regardless of exact revenue.

- **Value Before Revenue**
  - Revenue is treated as secondary in the early phase; the primary business objective is:
    - Clear evidence that the product is genuinely useful.
    - Willingness from at least some users to pay something for it once they see value.

### Key Performance Indicators

- **Lead Management KPIs**
  - `% of active leads with a tracked status and next action`  
    - Target: ≥ 80–90% for healthy users.
  - `Number of leads without any next action older than X days`  
    - Target: trending toward very low.

- **Usage / Habit KPIs**
  - `Workday Active Use` – percentage of workdays where a user opens the app and views the dashboard (leads + tasks).  
    - Target: majority of workdays for engaged users.
  - `Weekly Accounting Use` – percentage of weeks where the user logs new expenses or updates financial data.  
    - Target: most weeks for engaged users.

- **Financial Clarity KPIs**
  - `Monthly Review Completion Rate` – percentage of months where the user completes a monthly review (income, expenses by category, net worth).  
    - Target: at least 1 review per month for engaged users.
  - `User-reported confidence in finances` (simple internal rating or feedback), collected qualitatively at first.

- **Early Business KPIs**
  - `Number of active users (including you)` – expecting a small but committed group (1–5) in the first year.
  - `Retention after 3 months` – number of users still actively using NextStack ERP for both work tracking and accounting after 3 months.
  - Optional: `Number of users who say they would be willing to pay` for the product after using it.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

#### 1. Lead & Proposal Management (Minimum)

- Simple **Lead** doctype with:
  - Basic information: client/name, title/short description, estimated value (optional).
  - Source (e.g., Upwork, direct, other).
  - Status (e.g., New, In Discussion, Won, Lost).
  - Next action text field and optional next action date.
- Ability to:
  - Create/update leads.
  - See a list of open leads with upcoming next actions.
  - Store a simple link/reference to the Upwork job/client or external URL.

#### 2. Projects & Tasks (Minimum)

- **Project**:
  - Name.
  - Description of the project.
  - Link to client/lead where relevant.
  - Optionally, an attachment or supporting-notes area in a sidebar.
- **Task**:
  - Task subject.
  - Description/details.
  - Status (e.g., Open, In Progress, Completed).
  - Timeline fields (e.g., start date and due date or target date).
  - Link to parent project.
- Simple views:
  - List of projects.
  - List of tasks filtered by project and by status.

#### 3. Accounting Foundation

- **Chart of Accounts** appropriate for a freelancer/small agency.
- **GL Entry** doctype:
  - Separate GL Entry records individual transactions (similar to ERPNext GL Entry, but tailored to this product).
  - Each GL Entry line stores account, debit/credit, date, reference, etc.
- **Journal Entry**:
  - Allows manual recording of transactions that post to the GL Entry table.
- **Sales Invoice**:
  - Records income from clients.
  - On submit, creates corresponding GL Entries.
- **Purchase Invoice**:
  - Records expenses (tagged by category where possible).
  - On submit, creates corresponding GL Entries.
- **Payment**:
  - Records payments made/received against invoices.
  - Updates related GL Entries.
- **Basic General Ledger View**:
  - Ability to filter GL entries by account and date range to see what happened in a given period.

#### 4. Platform & Architecture (MVP)

- **Desktop-first application**:
  - Local database storage on the desktop as the primary source of truth.
- **Mobile and Sync (MVP framing)**:
  - Architecture planned for optional sync with mobile via Google Drive using Google login and Drive permissions.
  - MVP focuses on desktop implementation first; sync and mobile can start minimal and evolve after the desktop core is stable.

### Out of Scope for MVP

- Full, polished Upwork API integration (automatic lead/proposal import and status sync); MVP relies on manual entry and simple linking.
- Advanced role-based permissions comparable to full ERPNext (beyond simple roles/visibility for VA and bookkeeper).
- Complex, highly visual tree/grid project UI with advanced filters and custom views.
- Advanced analytics and dashboards beyond basic lists and simple GL/ledger views.
- Multi-tenant SaaS infrastructure; MVP can assume a single-user/small-user setup with local-first data.

### MVP Success Criteria

- You are able to:
  - Track **80–90% of your leads and proposals** in the system with status and next action.
  - Manage current projects and tasks using the built-in Project/Task structure.
  - Record income and expenses (Sales Invoices, Purchase Invoices, Payments, Journal Entries) so that:
    - The General Ledger for a month accurately reflects actual transactions.
- You complete:
  - Daily checks of leads/tasks from the desktop app on workdays.
  - At least weekly accounting updates and one monthly financial review using the GL and invoice data.
- The system feels like a supportive tool, not an extra burden, and gives you:
  - Better organization.
  - Clearer financial picture.
  - Real experience building and using your own product.

### Future Vision

- **Stronger Upwork integration**:
  - Automatic import of leads, proposals, contracts, and possibly messages.
  - Smarter insights on proposal performance and repeat clients.
- **Richer project/task UI**:
  - Tree/grid views similar to your reference screenshot.
  - More advanced filtering, grouping, and progress tracking.
- **Deeper mobile experience**:
  - Mobile app for fast expense entry, checking pending work, and light lead updates.
  - Reliable Google Drive–based sync between desktop and mobile.
- **Role-based access and collaboration**:
  - Fine-grained permissions for VA, bookkeeper, and team members (per doctype or module).
- **Expanded analytics and reports**:
  - More sophisticated dashboards for profitability by client/project, proposal conversion, and long-term net worth trends.
- **Support for more channels and larger agencies**:
  - Additional lead sources beyond Upwork.
  - Features tailored to multi-member teams and more complex agency workflows.
