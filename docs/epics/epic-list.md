# Epic List

## Epic 1: Invoice & Accounting Core

Provide complete workflows for issuing invoices, recording payments and expenses, and maintaining a correct general ledger suitable for a freelancer or tiny agency.

**FRs covered:** FR1, FR2, FR3, FR4, FR5, FR6, FR7, FR8, FR11, FR12, FR13, FR14, FR15, FR16, FR17, FR18, FR36.

## Epic 2: Money Cockpit & Receivables View

Give the user a clear, drill-downable view of how money is flowing, what is owed, and how things are trending over time.

**FRs covered:** FR9, FR10, FR19, FR20, FR21.

## Epic 3: Desktop Workspace, Sync & Configuration

Deliver a reliable local-first desktop app that runs comfortably on the user’s machines, stays in sync via Google Drive, and can be configured for their workflow and simple team roles.

**FRs covered:** FR28, FR29, FR30, FR31, FR32, FR33, FR34, FR35, FR37.

## Epic 4: Leads & Pipeline (Post-MVP)

Allow the user to track leads and follow-ups in a structured pipeline so they stop losing opportunities in Upwork and elsewhere.

**FRs covered:** FR22, FR23, FR24.

## Epic 5: Projects & Work Management (Post-MVP)

Let the user organize work into projects and tasks and connect that work back to invoices and expenses.

**FRs covered:** FR25, FR26, FR27.
