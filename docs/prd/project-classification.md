## Project Classification

**Technical Type:**  
Cross‑platform desktop application (Windows, macOS, Linux) with a future mobile companion, built with Tauri + React, local SQLite storage, and Google Drive–based sync.

**Domain:**  
General software – positioned explicitly as a “freelancer / small‑agency business OS” that connects leads, work, and accounting.

**Complexity:**  
Low regulatory complexity (no special healthcare/fintech/govtech constraints), but **moderate system complexity** driven by:  
- Offline‑first, multi‑device synchronization and conflict handling.  
- Double‑entry accounting, GL integrity, and basic financial reporting.  
- Multi‑surface UX (desktop first, future mobile) that must stay fast and approachable for non‑technical users.

**Project Context:**  
Greenfield – a new product designed from scratch, informed by previous idea documents and the product brief rather than an existing codebase.
